# 🇮🇳 AdhikarAI – AI-Powered Citizen Empowerment Platform

**Making government schemes, citizen rights, and essential information accessible to everyone.**

AdhikarAI is an AI-powered citizen empowerment platform designed to help people discover government schemes and scholarships, understand their eligibility, prepare required documents, identify potential scams, and access reliable information about their rights.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between citizens and the information they need through an accessible, user-friendly, and multilingual experience.

## 🌟 Problem Statement

Many citizens face challenges when trying to access government services and welfare schemes:

- Difficulty finding relevant government schemes and scholarships.
- Confusion about eligibility criteria and application procedures.
- Lack of clarity about required documents.
- Increasing exposure to fraudulent messages, links, and notices.
- Limited access to reliable information about citizen rights.
- Language barriers that make essential information difficult to understand.

## 💡 Our Solution

AdhikarAI brings essential citizen-support services together in one platform, using AI-assisted guidance to make information easier to discover, understand, and act upon.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Scheme & Scholarship Discovery
Helps users explore relevant government schemes and scholarships based on their needs and profile.

### 2. Document Doctor
Assists users in understanding document requirements and identifying potential issues in their documents.

### 3. Scam Shield
Helps users assess suspicious messages, links, and notices to improve awareness of potential fraud.

### 4. Know Your Rights
Provides accessible information about important citizen rights, legal awareness, and public services.

### 5. Multilingual AI Assistant
Offers conversational assistance to make information more accessible across supported languages.

### 6. Smart Profiler & Scheme Dashboard
Helps users organize relevant profile information and view schemes suited to their needs.

## 🎯 Sustainable Development Goals

AdhikarAI aims to contribute to the following United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

- **SDG 1 – No Poverty:** Improving awareness of welfare schemes and social protection.
- **SDG 4 – Quality Education:** Making scholarship information easier to discover.
- **SDG 10 – Reduced Inequalities:** Improving access to essential information.
- **SDG 16 – Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions:** Promoting awareness of rights and access to trustworthy information.
